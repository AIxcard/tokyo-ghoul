Tokyo Ghoul

東京喰種 トーキョーグール
Story & Art by Sui Ishida

In Tokyo, flesh-eating ghouls hide among humans. Shy college student Ken Kaneki goes on a date with Rize Kamishiro, unaware she is a ghoul. After an accident leads to her organs being transplanted into him, Kaneki becomes a half-ghoul, struggling to retain his humanity in a brutal conflict between ghouls and investigators.
